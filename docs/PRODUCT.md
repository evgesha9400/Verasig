# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- Primary users are crypto traders who use Telegram signal groups and need evidence before trusting a provider.
- Secondary users are signal channel owners who want an evidence-backed performance page.
- Initial internal users are operators who run ingestion, review parser gaps, label examples, and inspect score evidence.
- Users are not institutional quant teams, generic SRE teams, or non-technical retail investors seeking financial advice.

## Product Purpose

Verasig captures Telegram trading signal history, extracts recognized trade intent, simulates observe-only outcomes against market data, preserves deletion evidence, and scores providers from reconstructable proof.

Success means a trader or operator can identify good, bad, or untradeable signal sources from stored evidence without relying on provider claims.

## Brand Personality

- Forensic calm.
- Evidence-first.
- Precise.
- Restrained.
- Risk-aware.

## Anti-references

- Do not look like a hype-driven trading bot, exchange casino, or profit-promise funnel.
- Do not imply guaranteed returns, investment advice, or autonomous live execution.
- Do not hide score inputs behind black-box summaries.
- Do not treat social proof, subscribers, reactions, or views as quality claims without labeling them as proxies.
- Do not use decorative dashboard chrome that competes with evidence, tables, traces, and audit trails.

## Design Principles

- Evidence before opinion.
- Every score explains itself.
- Raw capture is the source of truth.
- Parser gaps are product signals.
- Observe-only until proof justifies promotion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG AA for future product surfaces.
- Maintain strong contrast for dense scorecards, tables, traces, and status labels.
- Support keyboard navigation across dashboard workflows.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Avoid color-only meaning for profit, loss, warning, confidence, and deletion evidence.
