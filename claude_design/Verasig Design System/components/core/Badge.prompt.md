Status badge — mono, uppercase, pill. Use for verification states and terse statuses.

```jsx
<Badge tone="verified" dot>Verified</Badge>
<Badge tone="pending" dot>Awaiting quorum</Badge>
<Badge tone="rejected" dot>Spoofed</Badge>
<Badge tone="info">Observing</Badge>
<Badge tone="outline">v2.4</Badge>
```

Tones: `neutral | verified | pending | rejected | info | outline | solid`. `dot` adds a leading status dot.
